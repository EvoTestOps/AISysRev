AISysRev

LLM-assisted systematic literature reviews.

AISysRev is a research-based tool for accelerating systematic literature reviews (SLRs). Import a list of papers, define inclusion and exclusion criteria, and choose from various LLMs from multiple providers to evaluate papers alongside your own manual screening.

  • Title-abstract screeningBinary decisions, 1–7 ratings or inclusion probabilities per criterion.
  • Full-text screeningUpload PDFs or bulk import from Zotero; the most relevant passages are matched to each criterion.
  • Multiple LLMsCompare models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Mistral and more via OpenRouter.
  • Import and exportCSV import from Scopus or GitHub; export results for Excel, R or Python.

Prompting strategies

Zero-shot The LLM screens each paper using only your inclusion and exclusion criteria.
Few-shot Papers you have labelled as include or exclude are added to the prompt as examples.
Per-criteria Each criterion is evaluated in its own LLM call for a more focused decision.

Based on research presented at FSE 2026: AISysRev – LLM-based Tool for Title-abstract Screening. Developed by the EvoTestOps group at the University of Helsinki.
